At what point should I consider professional home care?

When the physical demands are affecting your own health, when your loved one's needs exceed what you can safely manage alone, or when loneliness and isolation are becoming a real concern. Professional care does not have to be full-time; even a few hours a week can make a significant difference to both of you. Speak to an expert caregiver today.

What is the difference between personal care and companion care?

Personal care covers the physical daily tasks such as bathing, grooming, dressing, meal preparation, and medication reminders.

Companion care covers the emotional and social side, conversation, activities, and the consistent presence that prevents loneliness. A good care plan includes both.

How often should I review the home safety setup?

Every six months as a baseline, plus immediately after any fall or near-miss, and after any significant change in your loved one's condition. The emergency go bag specifically should be reviewed every three to six months.

What brain activities work best for someone with early memory loss?

Activities with no "wrong answer" tend to work best, such as photo reminiscing, This or That questions, storytelling, sorting activities, drawing, and colouring.

Jigsaw puzzles and card games also work well when the complexity is matched to their current level. The goal is engagement, not performance.
