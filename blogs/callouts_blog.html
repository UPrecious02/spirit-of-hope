🕐 Best Times to Give Medication

☀️
Morning
Best time: after breakfast. Many medications require food. Anchor to the morning meal to build a habit.
🌤️
Midday
Anchor to lunch. Set a phone alarm. This is the most commonly missed dose — an alert is not optional.
🌙
Evening
After dinner. Keep evening medications separate from morning ones to prevent confusion on difficult days.
😴
Bedtime
Bedtime medications include many sleep aids and certain heart medications. Never take with alcohol.
